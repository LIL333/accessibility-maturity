Before you start

This assessment contains 16 questions covering strategy, design, development, testing, content, compliance, and culture. For each question, choose the description that best matches your project's current state.

📌 How to choose your answer:
  • If you haven't fully reached the next level, select the level where most criteria have already been fulfilled — not the aspirational one.
  • If you feel you're in between two levels, choose whichever one is most applicable overall.
⚠️ Please note: This questionnaire provides a high-level self-assessment only. To confirm your actual maturity level with confidence, we recommend conducting structured interviews with your team and gathering as much supporting detail and evidence as possible.

It takes about 8–10 minutes. Your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to export them.
